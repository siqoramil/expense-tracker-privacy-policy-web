Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Expense Tracker mobile application ("the app", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information. By using the app, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — your email address, name, and a password (stored as a one-way bcrypt hash). If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google email and name only.
  • Profile picture — optional, chosen from your device's photo library. Stored on our server.
  • Financial data — transactions, budgets, debts, wallets, categories, recurring entries, and notifications that you create within the app. This data is stored on our server and linked to your account.
  • App preferences — language, currency, and theme settings.
  • Verification codes — temporary 6-digit codes sent to your email for password reset, email change, or account deletion. Codes expire within 10 minutes.

We do not collect: location data, contacts, microphone, camera, photos other than the one you explicitly select, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, or analytics.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and authenticate your account.
  • To store and sync your financial entries across devices.
  • To send transactional emails (verification codes for password reset, email change, account deletion).
  • To enforce rate limits and protect against abuse.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sell it to third parties.

3. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on our server hosted at expensetracker.ovrin.uz. Communication between the app and the server uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes; we cannot read them. Authentication uses signed JWT tokens.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Sign-In (optional) — if you sign in with Google, we receive your verified email and name from Google. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Email delivery — verification codes are delivered via a third-party email provider for transactional purposes only.

5. Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from within the app: Profile → Delete account. After confirming with a 6-digit code sent to your email, your user record and all associated data (transactions, budgets, debts, wallets, categories, recurring entries, notifications, verification codes) are permanently deleted from our server. This action cannot be undone.

Alternatively, you may request deletion by emailing siqoramil@gmail.com.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent change. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance.

8. Contact

If you have any questions, contact us at siqoramil@gmail.com.
